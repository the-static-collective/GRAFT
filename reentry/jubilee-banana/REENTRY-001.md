## Historical correction: recover the question before the name

- **July 20:** chat was described as ephemeral *weather*; a real IdeaVersion required a human HarvestEvent. Seed / Trace / Tension / Harvest, unresolved paths and compost mattered more than an infinitely updated summary. This was not yet durable Supabase proof.
- **July 26–28:** the BananaGram line began with literal household food and children, especially getting simple meals to families. Telegram chat was desirable because ordinary people already knew how to use it. The initial UI had a Shared Basket, seeds, pantry rescue, household chat and a Forest View; some receipts were simulated in localStorage and were **not** SHA-256. **Do not call the July UI a working bot.**
- **July 27–29:** Jubilee-Campfire formulated **Offer → Join → Remember** and portable `jubilee://` addresses. Real pledge semantics separate proposed, accepted, reported-complete, and confirmed. Campfire originally described a **small private circle of up to four**, not a public directory. The later `circle` product is a recognized descendant/donor, not magically a joined runtime.
- **July 29–August 2:** **NanaSpork** became the Android *field instrument*, **Garden** the humane home, **BananaGram** the portable seed/envelope, **Campfire** the privacy/relationship scope, **Jubilee** the authority/durable-memory kernel, and **Donkey** a device-private pause/translation tool. The Covenant Circuit explicitly separated **permission → stewardship → fulfillment**. Child welfare was purpose, not data to track.
- **August–September:** BananaSpork got runnable code, tests, a Capacitor/Android build path, Supabase-backed scoped needs, later member-scoped Realtime chat and Help Slip HOLD/POUR. Field two-account/RLS and physical provision remained unproven. `gramfork` explicitly narrowed the two-account need-to-confirmation proof; its README forbids pretending that a demo actor switch is identity.
- **August 24–September 19:** the Banana ELF is not a mascot for a permanent agent. A historical thought carries a **hatch-spec**; a fresh, mortal ELF receives local authority, performs bounded work, receipts, and ends. Banana Relay leaves **fruit, seed, compost** and zero successor authority. Banana ELF Time distinguishes seven-phase rest, 49 held phases and explicit 50th release. A seven-day meeting is **not** automatically a religious/Jubilee release.
- **September 26–October 4:** Telegram was re-scoped as **the porch, not the house**. The real Telegram Web K fork `BnanaGram` is a separate GPL-v3-derived UI substrate. `gramfork` PRs #1–#3 contain a read-only TenetGram porch and a bounded, executable Telegram webhook/Mini App identity-link proof—but no evidenced deployed bot and no canonical commands from Telegram. Transport identity never grants Jubilee actor, circle, or fulfillment authority.
- **October 8–25:** Jubilee Treasury added experimental signed/portable needs, multi-asset capacity and Penny physical/digital cases in open PRs; nothing is minted, funded or settled by that history. October Garden Signal / Seven-Day Table / Milk Bridge / Next Table are newer, separate practical civic surfaces. They must not silently import a household circle's private case, receipts, children, contacts, or pledges.

**Recovered spellings:** NanaSpork is the Android field instrument implemented in `BananaSpork`; `BananaGram-Gram-to-Fork` is a historical Messenger/food prototype; `gramfork` is a separate hardened authority descendant; `BnanaGram` is the Telegram Web-derived code body. `NanaGraph` / `Nanagram` are unverified as independent present-day repositories: do not invent a product merely to fill a naming gap. `Forest View` and `Free Graph` have different constitutional meanings.

## The cross-smash: a civic braid, not a mega-app

```text
LemonPRESS / paper / Telegram / SMS-like invitation / Garden
             (express a question; transport only)
                           │
                           ▼
         Seven-Day Table / two guests / ambassador
          (temporary consent-scoped civic table)
                           │
             question / offer / refusal
                           │
                           ▼
           BananaGram-style portable proposal
             (not a need.opened receipt)
                           │
                  human chooses one
                     /         \
                  HOLD       OFFER to steward
                              │
                              ▼
           First Yes / Milk Bridge permission gate
                         │
           steward-owned scope: CAMPFIRE/CIRCLE
           (only if real membership authorized)
                         │
                 lawful action/report
                         │
                 human confirmation
                         │
                 domain-owned receipt
                         │
           Banana Relay: FRUIT / SEED / COMPOST
                         │
               GRAFT / Free Graph indexes
             authorized public-safe summary
                         │
                  LemonPRESS / Next Table
                (new invitation, not inheritance)
```

**Most important correction:** the Seven-Day Table's eight founding invitations plus sixteen possible referrals are **not** one Jubilee four-person Campfire. One is an open-time civic convening experiment, the other is a bounded private relationship/authority scope. A bridge requires explicit human selection, authentication where necessary, membership proof, and context/consent limits. An ambassador may carry a question but cannot transfer a steward's institution-level or household-level authority.

**Temporal correction:** 7 days at the table can trigger a *review and pause*, not automatic close/harvest/receipt/50th Jubilee release. A new table is a new invitation; Banana Relay seeds have **zero** authority to start it.

**Telegram correction:** v0 can distribute a link and read a prompt. A real bot can be added *after* two-account gramfork proof; then verified webhook `porch.intent/v0` must remain inert until separately authenticated human review. No command autocomplete may open a need, accept a pledge, notify a stranger, or write a receipt by itself.

**Household protection:** NanaSpork Donkey drafts never become indexable GRAFT data or Telegram summaries. No child identity, location, aid case, allergy, custody situation, photograph or private crisis moves into public Free Graph/LemonPRESS. The earliest purpose was feeding actual people, not scoring their provision or extracting their private lives.

## One executable specimen we could use this week

**Question:** Did the food shelf's reported temporary milk support actually create a purchasing gap?

1. **Human ask:** ask the food shelf purchaser whether one narrow comparison would be useful. A no ends this attempt.
2. **Fictional door:** Case 002 / Vanishing Carton teaches that promised, quoted, delivered and confirmed are different facts.
3. **Temporary table:** each willing, named invitee may contribute asynchronously, nominate two others or send an ambassador; the host does not assume anyone enrolled.
4. **Constrain:** one permissioned item, source and unit; verify current program, cost, schedule and refrigeration **before** seeking suppliers.
5. **One mortal ELF-shaped task:** generate a printable comparison template, with 1 bounded action and no credentials or external execution by default.
6. **Honest return:** annotate FRUIT (actually prepared/completed), SEED (one possible next question), COMPOST (failed assumptions, no answer, refusal).
7. **Selective public share:** after agency approval, LemonPRESS may print the public-safe lesson; the next table gets a new optional invitation, not the buyer's private case.

**Valid outcomes:** best existing option; no actionable gap; a verified comparison; authorized new introduction; declined; not yet known. **Invalid outcome:** “24 cartons rescued” based only on a conversation, game, quote, pledge or self-report.
