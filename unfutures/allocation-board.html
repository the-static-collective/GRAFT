The hidden difference

At a budget of seven kits, the largest-remainder rule yields 3, 3, 1; declared-order remainder yields 4, 3, 0.

Both conserve exactly seven units. But the second policy gives a fictional program zero. The policy is part of the receipt, not an invisible implementation detail.

A zero allocation does not automatically establish injustice, nor does a positive allocation establish adequacy. Affected people must be involved in selecting any real policy, evidence, minimum obligations, and exception process.
