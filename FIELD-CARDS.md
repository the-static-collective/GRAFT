# WEAVES → WE'VES 001 — paper-first participation cards
**Four reusable blanks · no account, no app, no automatic publication**

Print this file or copy the questions onto paper. These are **human work surfaces**, not a consent waiver, legal intake form, social score, partner registration or evidence of completed work.

---

### One fictional field example

A household says it could use a grocery run. A nearby adult says they might have time. A third person offers a paper invitation card. This creates a **possible weave**. No one has been assigned or enrolled.

If separately willing adults agree, safeguards are checked, the action occurs and the recipient independently confirms it, there may be a **we've**: *we've helped deliver groceries*. Someone else can then **reweave** that public-safe lesson into a new voluntary idea—without inheriting the household's identity, contact details or any obligation.

**Hand this back to the local steward first. Only a separately approved non-identifying public projection should enter GRAFT.**
